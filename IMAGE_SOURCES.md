or representations of verified locations, people or achievements. They require no
third-party attribution. SVG keeps these illustrations crisp and below 3 KB each;
the photographic 120–250 KB target does not apply to this vector fallback.

Replace them with locally optimized licensed photographs when supplied. Centre

contain verified, consented student information; no identities, grades or ranks
